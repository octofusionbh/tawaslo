For agencies · powered by Tawaslo

Run your clients' venues from one cockpit.

Agencies manage their clients' social, orders and bookings in one place — invited in by the venue, revoked with a click. The management side is called Tawaslo.

1

The venue invites you

Your client opens Orderlo, generates a connection code, and shares it with you. They stay in control the whole time.

2

You connect in Tawaslo

Paste the code into that client's portal. You're now a manager on their venue — menu, orders, reservations, all of it.

3

They can revoke, any time

The venue owns their data. One click removes your access, and they keep every order, guest and setting.

One place for social and service.

Manage a client's Instagram and their pickup orders on the same screen. Switch between clients without switching tools. No spreadsheets, no shared passwords, no guesswork.

Connect by code, not by API key. A leaked code expires; a removed agency loses access instantly. Simple for the venue, safe for everyone.
