Talk to us

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I need a card to start?

No. The 14-day trial needs no card. You only pay if you decide to keep going.

Is there any hardware?

None. Orderlo runs in the browser and over WhatsApp — no tablets, no installer, no cables.

Whose name do the WhatsApp messages come from?

Yours. Messages go out under your venue's name. You can start on our shared number and move to your own whenever you like.

What about WhatsApp costs?

Replies within the 24-hour customer window are free; you only pay WhatsApp's small per-message fee for some business-initiated messages. Most order and reminder messages fall in the free window.

Can my agency run it for me?

Yes — you can invite an agency in with a code and revoke it with a click, any time. See For agencies.
