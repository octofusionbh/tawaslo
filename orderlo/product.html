What it does

Everything the front of house needs — nothing it doesn't.

Five jobs, one system, no hardware. Here's exactly what Orderlo handles while your team looks after the room.

01

Your menu, live and shareable

Type or upload your menu or products, prices and photos. Guests open it from a QR on the table or a link in your bio — you change it whenever the kitchen does.

02

Orders and pickup, straight from chat

Customers order for pickup in the same WhatsApp thread. Each order lands on your board with the items, the time and any notes — nothing lost on a napkin.

03

Reservations without the back-and-forth

Take table bookings or appointment slots, send confirmations and reminders, and quietly cut the no-shows that empty your Fridays.

04

Loyalty that runs itself

Reward the regulars and nudge the ones who've gone quiet — automatically, with no punch cards and no spreadsheet.

05

An AI host that never clocks out

Trained on your venue, it answers questions, takes orders and books tables at 2am in Arabic or English — and hands anything tricky to your team.
