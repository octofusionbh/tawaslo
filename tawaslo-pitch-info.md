## 1. Company snapshot
- **Name:** Tawaslo (from the Arabic *tawāsol* — "communication / connection").
- **What it is:** an **Arabic-first, all-in-one social media management + customer-engagement platform** for the Gulf (GCC). One dashboard to plan, create, schedule and publish social content, handle messages, run ads, and use WhatsApp — with AI doing the heavy lifting.
- **Company:** an **Octo Fusion** product. Based in **Manama, Bahrain**.
- **Who it's for:** marketing **agencies** (manage many clients), **brands / enterprises** (manage their own accounts), **freelancers**, and **F&B / retail venues**.
- **One-liner:** *"One Arabic-first platform to run all your social, WhatsApp and customer engagement."*

## 2. The problem
- Gulf businesses and agencies juggle many disconnected tools for social media, content, DMs, ads and WhatsApp.
- Almost every tool is **English-first** and handles Arabic content, GCC context, and **WhatsApp** (the region's #1 channel) poorly or not at all.
- Small businesses (cafés, shops, salons) can't afford enterprise tools or dedicated social teams.
- Result: wasted hours, missed messages, inconsistent posting, and no single view of performance.

## 3. The solution
Tawaslo puts it all in **one Arabic-first dashboard**: plan a calendar, generate content with AI, schedule and auto-publish across platforms, get client approvals, reply to every comment and DM in one inbox, run and boost ads, and use WhatsApp — plus a built-in AI Concierge and a full venue toolkit.

## 4. Product & features (all built / live unless noted)

**Content & publishing**
- Publisher — post to all connected platforms from one place.
- Planner & Calendar — plan a content calendar, drag-and-drop scheduling, auto-publish.
- Approvals — clients review & approve posts before they go live.
- Shareable client calendar — send each client a clean view of upcoming content.
- Carousels & multi-image posts, Stories.
- Media library, Trending content, best-time-to-post guidance.

**Platforms**
- **Live:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.
- **Rolling out / in review:** LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube, Google Business Profile.

**AI Studio**
- AI-generated captions & content in **Arabic + English**.
- Brand Voice — trained per client so AI writes in that brand's tone.
- AI Reply Assistant for the inbox.

**Engagement**
- Unified Inbox — comments + DMs across platforms in one place.
- Social Listening — track keywords & mentions.
- Crisis Radar — alerts on negative-sentiment spikes.

**Ads**
- Ads Manager — create, run and **boost paid campaigns** (Meta), with per-ad and all-ads reporting.

**Analytics & Reports**
- Dashboards + exportable reports (social; plus reservations & pickup for venues), month-over-month growth, AI insights.

**WhatsApp & AI Concierge**
- Automated customer messaging + marketing **broadcasts**.
- **AI Concierge** — a trained AI host that answers customers, takes orders and books tables/appointments **24/7 in Arabic & English**.

**Agency / client growth**
- Clients management, Win Clients, Suggested leads, Campaigns.
- Team invites, roles & permissions.

**Venue / F&B suite**
- Digital menu, QR ordering, pickup orders, reservations, loyalty, reviews, guest CRM, Fill My Tables.

**Business layer**
- Billing & plans, Settings, and an HQ admin dashboard (revenue/MRR, usage, roles).
